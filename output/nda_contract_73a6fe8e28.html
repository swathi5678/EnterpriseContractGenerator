NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement"), effective as of **[Insert Effective Date]** (the "Effective Date"), is made and entered into by and between:

Acme Corp.

A corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of the State of **[Acme Corp's State of Incorporation]**, with its principal place of business located at **[Acme Corp's Address]

(hereinafter referred to as "Disclosing Party");

AND

John Doe

An individual residing at **[John Doe's Address]

(hereinafter referred to as "Receiving Party").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Disclosing Party is engaged in the business of **[briefly describe Acme Corp's business, e.g., technology development, software solutions, etc.]** and possesses valuable confidential and proprietary information;

WHEREAS, Receiving Party is an independent consultant specializing in Artificial Intelligence (AI) and has been engaged by Disclosing Party to provide AI consulting services for a period of twelve (12) months (the "Purpose");

WHEREAS, in connection with the Purpose, Disclosing Party may disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to Receiving Party, and Receiving Party may gain access to such information;

WHEREAS, the parties desire to protect the confidentiality of such information in accordance with the terms and conditions set forth herein.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

AGREEMENT

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public information, whether commercial, financial, technical, operational, or otherwise, disclosed by Disclosing Party to Receiving Party, directly or indirectly, in writing, orally, electronically, visually, or by any other means, or to which Receiving Party gains access, in connection with the Purpose.

Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

a. **Technical Information:** Data, designs, algorithms, software, source code, object code, AI models, machine learning models, neural networks, training data, datasets, architectures, specifications, schematics, prototypes, inventions, discoveries, research, development, and intellectual property.

b. **Business Information:** Business plans, strategies, financial information, forecasts, projections, customer lists, potential customer information, marketing plans, sales data, pricing strategies, supplier information, personnel information, and operational methodologies.

c. **Proprietary Processes:** Proprietary processes, methodologies, know-how, trade secrets, and any other information related to Disclosing Party's AI projects, products, services, or internal operations.

d. **Designation:** Any information that is marked or otherwise designated as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or with a similar legend, or that, by its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure, would reasonably be understood by a reasonable person to be confidential or proprietary.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

Receiving Party hereby agrees to:

a. **Maintain Confidentiality:** Maintain all Confidential Information in strict confidence and prevent its unauthorized disclosure, publication, or dissemination.

b. **Limited Use:** Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose of providing AI consulting services to Disclosing Party and for no other purpose whatsoever.

c. **Non-Disclosure:** Not disclose, reproduce, copy, transmit, or otherwise make available any Confidential Information to any third party without the prior written consent of Disclosing Party.

d. **Standard of Care:** Exercise at least the same degree of care to prevent the unauthorized disclosure or use of Confidential Information as Receiving Party uses to protect its own confidential or proprietary information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

e. **Restricted Access:** Limit access to Confidential Information to those of its employees, contractors, or agents who have a strict "need-to-know" such information for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations substantially similar to those contained herein. Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its employees, contractors, or agents.

3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

The obligations of confidentiality set forth in this Agreement shall not apply to any information that Receiving Party can demonstrate:

a. Is or becomes publicly available through no fault or breach of this Agreement by Receiving Party;

b. Was rightfully known to Receiving Party prior to its disclosure by Disclosing Party, without any obligation of confidentiality;

c. Is independently developed by Receiving Party without the use of or reference to Disclosing Party's Confidential Information;

d. Is rightfully received by Receiving Party from a third party without restriction on disclosure and without breach of any confidentiality obligation owed to Disclosing Party; or

e. Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or a valid order of a court or governmental agency, provided that Receiving Party (where legally permissible) gives Disclosing Party prompt prior written notice of such requirement to enable Disclosing Party to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

4. TERM AND SURVIVAL

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall continue in full force and effect for a period of **twelve (12) months**, unless terminated earlier by mutual written agreement of the parties or as otherwise provided herein.

Notwithstanding the expiration or termination of this Agreement, the obligations of confidentiality regarding Confidential Information shall survive for a period of **three (3) years** from the date of disclosure of such Confidential Information. For any information constituting a "trade secret" under applicable law, the obligations of confidentiality shall survive indefinitely, or for as long as such information remains a trade secret.

5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Upon the termination or expiration of this Agreement, or upon Disclosing Party's earlier written request, Receiving Party shall promptly:

a. Return to Disclosing Party all Confidential Information (including all copies, reproductions, summaries, and extracts thereof) in its possession or control; or

b. At Disclosing Party's option, destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies, reproductions, summaries, and extracts thereof) in its possession or control and certify such destruction in writing to Disclosing Party within ten (10) business days of such request.

Receiving Party may retain one (1) copy of the Confidential Information for archival purposes, subject to the continuing confidentiality obligations hereunder.

6. REMEDIES

Receiving Party acknowledges that monetary damages may be an inadequate remedy for any breach of this Agreement, and that Disclosing Party would suffer irreparable harm from such a breach. Accordingly, Receiving Party agrees that Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, or other equitable remedies, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the necessity of posting a bond or other security.

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the internal laws of the State of **[Acme Corp's State of Incorporation]**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties hereby consent to the exclusive jurisdiction of the state and federal courts located in **[County, State of Acme Corp's State of Incorporation]** for any action arising out of or relating to this Agreement.

8. MISCELLANEOUS

a. **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties relating to the confidentiality of information.

b. **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is found by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid, legal, and enforceable, and the remainder of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

c. **Waiver:** No waiver of any breach of any provision of this Agreement shall be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach or of any other provision. Any waiver must be in writing and signed by the party granting the waiver.

d. **Assignment:** Neither party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement, in whole or in part, without the prior written consent of the other party. Any attempted assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

e. **No License:** Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any rights, by license or otherwise, to Receiving Party in or to any Confidential Information, or any patent, copyright, trademark, or other intellectual property rights of Disclosing Party.

f. **Independent Contractors:** The parties are independent contractors, and nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as creating a partnership, joint venture, agency, or employment relationship between them.
