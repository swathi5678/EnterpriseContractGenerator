NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "Agreement") is made and entered into as of this [Day] day of [Month], [Year] (the "Effective Date"),

BY AND BETWEEN:

Acme Corp.**, a corporation duly organized and existing under the laws of [State/Country of Incorporation], with its principal place of business at [Acme Corp. Address] (hereinafter, "Acme" or "Disclosing Party"),

AND

John Doe**, an individual residing at [John Doe's Address] (hereinafter, "Consultant" or "Receiving Party").

(Each a "Party" and collectively, the "Parties")

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Acme is engaged in the business of [brief description of Acme's business, e.g., developing and deploying advanced AI solutions];

WHEREAS, Consultant possesses expertise in artificial intelligence and related fields;

WHEREAS, Acme and Consultant desire to explore or engage in a potential business relationship concerning a 12-month AI consulting engagement (the "Purpose"); and

WHEREAS, in connection with the Purpose, the Disclosing Party may disclose certain confidential and proprietary information to the Receiving Party, and the Receiving Party is willing to receive such information subject to the terms and conditions set forth herein.

AGREEMENT

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the Parties agree as follows:

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" shall mean any and all non-public information, data, or materials, whether commercial, financial, technical, operational, or otherwise, disclosed by Acme to Consultant, directly or indirectly, in writing, orally, visually, electronically, or by any other means, and whether or not marked as "confidential," "proprietary," or with a similar designation. Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

a. **AI-Specific Information:** Proprietary artificial intelligence models, algorithms, machine learning methodologies, neural network architectures, training datasets, data labeling techniques, inference engines, and related documentation or source code.

b. **Business Information:** Business plans, strategies, marketing plans, financial data, pricing information, customer lists, prospect lists, supplier information, employee data, and market research.

c. **Technical Information:** Product specifications, designs, schematics, software code (source and object), hardware configurations, research and development activities, inventions, discoveries, know-how, processes, and trade secrets.

d. **Operational Information:** Information related to Acme's operations, internal processes, security protocols, and intellectual property.

e. Any information that, by its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure, would reasonably be understood by a person of ordinary prudence to be confidential or proprietary to Acme.

2. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Confidential Information shall not include any information that:

a. Is or becomes generally available to the public through no fault or breach of this Agreement by the Receiving Party;

b. Was rightfully in the possession of the Receiving Party prior to its disclosure by Acme, as evidenced by written records;

c. Is independently developed by the Receiving Party without the use of or reference to Acme's Confidential Information, as evidenced by written records;

d. Is rightfully received by the Receiving Party from a third party without restriction on disclosure and without breach of any confidentiality obligation owed to Acme; or

e. Is required to be disclosed by law or by a court order, governmental agency, or other legal process, provided that the Receiving Party gives Acme prompt written notice of such requirement (where legally permissible) to enable Acme to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and cooperates with Acme's efforts to obtain such a remedy.

3. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

The Receiving Party agrees to:

a. **Non-Use:** Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose of the 12-month AI consulting engagement and for no other purpose whatsoever.

b. **Non-Disclosure:** Not disclose, publish, disseminate, or otherwise make available any Confidential Information to any third party without Acme's prior written consent.

c. **Protection:** Exercise at least the same degree of care to protect the Confidential Information as the Receiving Party uses to protect its own proprietary information of a similar nature, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

d. **Limited Access:** Restrict access to the Confidential Information to those of its employees, agents, or approved subcontractors who have a strict "need-to-know" such information for the Purpose and who are bound by written confidentiality obligations substantially similar to those contained herein. The Receiving Party shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its employees, agents, or subcontractors.

4. TERM OF AGREEMENT AND SURVIVAL OF OBLIGATIONS

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in full force and effect for a period of **three (3) years** from the Effective Date, regardless of the duration of the underlying AI consulting engagement.

Notwithstanding the foregoing, the confidentiality obligations set forth in Section 3 of this Agreement with respect to all Confidential Information disclosed during the 12-month AI consulting engagement shall survive the termination or expiration of this Agreement for a period of **five (5) years** from the date of such termination or expiration.

5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF INFORMATION

Upon Acme's written request, or upon the termination or expiration of the AI consulting engagement or this Agreement, whichever occurs first, the Receiving Party shall promptly return to Acme or, at Acme's option, destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies, reproductions, summaries, analyses, and extracts thereof) in its possession, custody, or control. The Receiving Party shall, within ten (10) business days of such request or event, provide Acme with a written certification signed by an authorized representative of the Receiving Party, confirming that all Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed as required herein.

6. NO LICENSE OR WARRANTY

Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting, by implication, estoppel, or otherwise, any license or rights under any patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, or other intellectual property rights of Acme. All Confidential Information shall remain the sole property of Acme. Acme makes no representations or warranties, express or implied, as to the accuracy, completeness, or suitability of the Confidential Information for any particular purpose.

7. REMEDIES

The Receiving Party acknowledges that monetary damages may not be a sufficient remedy for any breach of this Agreement, and that Acme may suffer irreparable harm as a result of such a breach. Therefore, in the event of any actual or threatened breach of this Agreement, Acme shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, or other equitable remedies, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual damages.

8. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of [State, e.g., Delaware], without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties agree that any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the federal or state courts located in [County, State, e.g., New Castle County, Delaware].

9. ENTIRE AGREEMENT

This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties relating to the confidentiality of information. No modification or amendment to this Agreement shall be effective unless made in writing and signed by duly authorized representatives of both parties.

10. MISCELLANEOUS

a. **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is found by a court of competent jurisdiction to be invalid, illegal, or unenforceable, such provision shall be modified to the minimum extent necessary to make it valid, legal, and enforceable, and the remainder of this Agreement shall remain in full force and effect.

b. **Waiver:** No failure or delay by either party in exercising any right, power, or privilege under this Agreement shall operate as a waiver thereof, nor shall any single or partial exercise thereof preclude any other or further exercise thereof or the exercise of any other right, power, or privilege.

c. **Assignment:** Neither party may assign or transfer its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. Any attempted assignment or transfer without such consent shall be null and void.

d. **Notices:** All notices required or permitted under this Agreement shall be in writing and shall be deemed effectively given upon personal delivery, confirmed facsimile transmission, or three (3) business days after deposit in the mail, postage prepaid, certified or registered, return receipt requested, to the addresses first set forth above or to such other address as either party may designate by written notice to the other.

e. **Counterparts:** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.
