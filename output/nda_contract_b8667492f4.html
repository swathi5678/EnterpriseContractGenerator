NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Non-Disclosure Agreement (this "Agreement"), effective as of **[Insert Effective Date]** (the "Effective Date"), is made and entered into by and between:

Acme Corp**, a corporation organized and existing under the laws of **[State/Country of Incorporation, e.g., Delaware]**, with its principal place of business at **[Acme Corp Full Address]** (hereinafter, "Acme" or "Disclosing Party");

AND

John Doe**, an individual residing at **[John Doe's Full Address]** (hereinafter, "Consultant" or "Receiving Party").

RECITALS

WHEREAS, Acme is engaging Consultant for a twelve (12) month AI consulting engagement (the "Purpose"); and

WHEREAS, in connection with the Purpose, Acme may disclose to Consultant certain confidential and proprietary information; and

WHEREAS, the parties desire to set forth the terms and conditions under which such confidential information will be disclosed and protected.

NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants and agreements contained herein, and for other good and valuable consideration, the receipt and sufficiency of which are hereby acknowledged, the parties agree as follows:

---

1. DEFINITION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

"Confidential Information" means any and all non-public, proprietary, or trade secret information, data, materials, or knowledge, whether oral, written, electronic, visual, or in any other form, disclosed by Acme to Consultant, directly or indirectly, in connection with the Purpose. Confidential Information includes, but is not limited to:

a. **Technical Information:** Acme's AI models, algorithms, machine learning architectures, datasets (including training, validation, and test data), source code, object code, software, hardware, designs, specifications, research and development, prototypes, inventions, discoveries, know-how, and other intellectual property.

b. **Business Information:** Business plans, strategies, financial data, pricing information, marketing plans, customer lists, customer information, supplier lists, operational methods, product development plans, and other proprietary business processes.

c. **Operational Information:** Information related to Acme's operations, products, services, personnel, and internal procedures.

d. **Designated Information:** Any information that is marked or otherwise designated as "Confidential," "Proprietary," or with a similar legend, or that, by its nature or the circumstances of its disclosure, would reasonably be understood to be confidential.

2. OBLIGATIONS OF RECEIVING PARTY

Consultant, as the Receiving Party, hereby agrees to:

a. **Maintain Confidentiality:** Hold all Confidential Information in strict confidence and exercise at least the same degree of care to prevent its unauthorized disclosure, publication, or dissemination as Consultant uses to protect its own confidential information, but in no event less than a reasonable degree of care.

b. **Non-Disclosure:** Not disclose, publish, or disseminate any Confidential Information to any third party without Acme's prior written consent.

c. **Limited Use:** Use the Confidential Information solely for the Purpose of the AI consulting engagement and for no other purpose whatsoever.

d. **Limited Access:** Limit access to Confidential Information to those of its employees, agents, or contractors who have a bona fide need to know such information for the Purpose and who are bound by confidentiality obligations substantially similar to those contained herein. Consultant shall be responsible for any breach of this Agreement by its employees, agents, or contractors.

3. EXCLUSIONS FROM CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

The obligations of confidentiality set forth in this Agreement shall not apply to any information that Consultant can demonstrate:

a. Was already publicly known or became publicly known through no fault of Consultant;

b. Was rightfully received by Consultant from a third party without restriction on disclosure and without breach of any confidentiality obligation;

c. Was independently developed by Consultant without use of or reference to Acme's Confidential Information; or

d. Is required to be disclosed by law, regulation, or court order, provided that Consultant gives prompt written notice to Acme of such requirement to allow Acme to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy, and Consultant cooperates with Acme in such efforts.

4. TERM AND SURVIVAL

This Agreement shall commence on the Effective Date and shall remain in full force and effect for the duration of the twelve (12) month AI consulting engagement. Notwithstanding the foregoing, the obligations of confidentiality and non-use set forth in Section 2 shall survive the termination or expiration of the consulting engagement for a period of three (3) years thereafter.

5. RETURN OR DESTRUCTION OF CONFIDENTIAL INFORMATION

Upon the termination or expiration of the consulting engagement, or upon Acme's earlier written request, Consultant shall promptly return to Acme or destroy all Confidential Information (including all copies, reproductions, and summaries thereof) in its possession or control, regardless of the medium in which it is stored. Consultant shall, upon Acme's request, certify in writing that all such Confidential Information has been returned or destroyed.

6. REMEDIES

Consultant acknowledges that monetary damages may be an insufficient remedy for any breach of this Agreement and that Acme shall be entitled to seek injunctive relief, specific performance, or other equitable remedies, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity, without the necessity of posting a bond or proving actual damages.

7. GOVERNING LAW

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of the State of **[Insert State, e.g., Delaware]**, without regard to its conflict of laws principles. The parties agree that any action or proceeding arising out of or relating to this Agreement shall be brought exclusively in the federal or state courts located in **[Insert County, State, e.g., New Castle County, Delaware]**.

8. MISCELLANEOUS

a. **Entire Agreement:** This Agreement constitutes the entire understanding and agreement between the parties concerning the subject matter hereof and supersedes all prior and contemporaneous agreements, understandings, negotiations, and discussions, whether oral or written, between the parties.

b. **No License:** Nothing in this Agreement shall be construed as granting any rights or licenses, express or implied, under any patents, copyrights, trademarks, trade secrets, or other intellectual property rights of Acme to Consultant.

c. **Assignment:** Neither party may assign its rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written consent of the other party. Any attempted assignment without such consent shall be null and void.

d. **Waiver:** No waiver of any provision of this Agreement shall be effective unless in writing and signed by the waiving party. A waiver of any breach shall not be deemed a waiver of any subsequent breach.

e. **Severability:** If any provision of this Agreement is found to be invalid or unenforceable, the remaining provisions shall remain in full force and effect.

f. **Headings:** The headings in this Agreement are for convenience only and shall not affect its interpretation.

g. **Counterparts:** This Agreement may be executed in counterparts, each of which shall be deemed an original, but all of which together shall constitute one and the same instrument.

h. **Relationship of Parties:** Nothing contained in this Agreement shall be construed as creating any agency, partnership, joint venture, or other form of joint enterprise, employment, or fiduciary relationship between the parties.

i. **Notices:** All notices, requests, consents, claims, demands, waivers, and other communications hereunder (each, a "Notice") must be in writing and addressed to the parties at the addresses set forth in the preamble of this Agreement (or to such other address that may be designated by the receiving party from time to time in accordance with this Section). All Notices must be delivered by personal delivery, nationally recognized overnight courier (with all fees pre-paid), or certified or registered mail (in each case, return receipt requested, postage pre-paid). Except as otherwise provided in this Agreement, a Notice is effective only (a) upon receipt by the receiving party, and (b) if the party giving the Notice has complied with the requirements of this Section.
